Common questions

What else should I know?

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You pay $99/month (or $864/year) and a 90/10 split until you reach your $5,900 annual cap. That's it. No franchise fees, no desk fees, no technology fees.

What happens when I hit my cap?

Once you pay $5,900 into the company through your 10% split, you keep 100% of your commissions for the rest of your anniversary year.

Do I have to use your coaching program?

No, coaching is completely optional. If you choose to participate, it's performance-based, meaning you only pay a percentage out of your closings when the coaching produces results.

How does the residual income work?

When you refer an agent to VPR, you earn a percentage of their closings. It's built into the 90/10 split and spans three tiers, giving you a way to build passive income just by helping grow the family.

Will my current broker find out if I inquire?

Absolutely not. All conversations are 100% confidential. We understand the sensitivity of making a move and respect your privacy completely.
